# Nourish & Flourish: setup (about 10 minutes, free)

## Put it online (GitHub Pages)
1. Make a free account at github.com.
2. Tap + > New repository. Name it `nourish-flourish`. Keep it Public. Create it.
3. Tap "uploading an existing file". Drag in everything from this folder: index.html, sw.js, manifest.webmanifest, and the icons folder. Commit.
4. Settings > Pages > Source: "Deploy from a branch", branch main, folder /root. Save.
5. After about a minute your link appears: https://YOURNAME.github.io/nourish-flourish/

Your health entries are never uploaded. The page only holds the app's code, not your data.

## Install on iPhone
1. Open your link in **Safari**.
2. Tap Share > Add to Home Screen > Add.
3. Open the app from the new pink icon, and use it from there from now on.
   (Safari and the Home Screen app keep separate data, so don't log in both.)

## Updating later
Replace the changed files in GitHub (Add file > Upload files). Open sw.js and change `nourish-flourish-v1` to `v2`, `v3`, and so on. Close and reopen the app twice to get the update. Your data stays put.

## Back up
More > Save backup > Save to Files (iCloud Drive). Do this every couple of weeks, and before deleting the app or clearing Safari data.
